How to use

  1. Rotate the model by dragging it.
  2. Zoom using pinch gestures or your mouse wheel.
  3. On a supported phone, tap "View in AR".
  4. Follow the phone's instructions to place the object.

AR requires a compatible mobile device and supported browser. On Android, Scene Viewer may open. On iPhone, Quick Look may open.
