How to use

  3. Open this website on a compatible smartphone.
  4. Tap View in AR and follow the on-screen instructions.

Android: use a compatible device and Chrome. iPhone: use Safari for Apple Quick Look. AR availability depends on your device, browser, and supported AR software.
