How to use

  1. Drag the model to rotate it.

  3. Open this website on a compatible smartphone.
  4. Tap View in AR and follow the on-screen instructions.

Android generally uses Google Scene Viewer. iPhone generally uses Apple Quick Look. AR availability depends on your device and browser.
